What it suggests:

- **Crawled, not indexed** pages: 8 of 12 are short (460–750 words), and most overlap stronger pages. The four longer ones (/health-benefits-of-quitting-smoking, /how-long-does-nicotine-stay-in-your-system, /quit-smoking-with-anxiety, /quit-smoking-with-depression, 1,100–1,240 words) were all last crawled before the 10-13 edit, so a recrawl request is worth trying for those. That's Google judging them not worth indexing. The fix is a refresh or a merge, not resubmitting.
- **Unknown/discovered** pages include some with many internal links (/prepare-to-quit-smoking has 30). That points at crawl budget or the sitemap being read rarely, as with the guides in BLO-24.
- **Indexed but invisible:** 16 indexed articles have 0 impressions in both windows, 15 of them flagged thin (428–690 words). Four comparison pages are in the same state. /nicotine-withdrawal-symptoms is the most valuable of these and is now BLO-40. The rest are under Watching.
