- Amplitude only sees a fraction of organic traffic (details under Analytics mismatches).

## Analytics mismatches

- Amplitude recorded 81 page views and 2 App Store clicks (/ and /guides) for the whole site. GSC alone sent 12 organic clicks. Guides that got GSC clicks (diarrhea 2, joint-pain 2, nausea 1, dizziness 1) show 0 views in Amplitude. Most organic visitors are probably never tracked, likely because of the cookie consent gate or ad blockers. Until this is fixed, Amplitude can't rank organic landing pages by conversion. Commented on BLO-5.
- `app_store_click` and `tool_input` have never been received in Amplitude. App Store clicks come only from `app_store_badge_click` and `cta_click`, and tool use (calculators) isn't measured at all. Commented on BLO-5.
