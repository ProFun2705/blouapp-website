All four pages are 500-650 words and flagged thin. Four refreshes filed (BLO-28 to BLO-31).

- **"Free nicotine patches" (2,900) belongs on the state quitline hub,** which already lists it as a keyword but isn't titled for it. Filed as BLO-32.
- **Amplitude can't weight by conversion yet.** 81 page views and 2 App Store clicks site-wide, so no cluster got the conversion +1.
