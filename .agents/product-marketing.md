# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-22

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** Quit smoking, tracked simply.

**What it does:** Blou is a quit-smoking tracker for iPhone. It makes progress visible (streak, money saved, lung recovery milestones) and helps interrupt cravings with a short timer plus a pre-committed 2-minute action. The website explains the app, converts App Store downloads, and publishes evidence-based quit guides, comparison pages, and localized money-saved calculators.

**Product category:** Quit-smoking tracker / smoking cessation app (health & fitness)

**Product type:** Mobile app (iOS) + marketing/content website

**Business model:** Free core tracking (streak, savings, cravings, milestones); optional premium. Website is free; primary monetization is App Store installs and in-app premium.

## Target Audience

**Target users:** Adults who smoke or vape, often on a phone, often in the first days of a quit or during a craving.

**Decision-makers:** Self-directed (B2C); no enterprise buyer.

**Primary use case:** Track a quit attempt with visible progress and fast craving support.

**Jobs to be done:**
- See whether the quit is working (streak, savings, milestones)
- Get through a craving without smoking (timer + replacement action)
- Understand withdrawal and recovery (guides, timelines, calculators)

**Use cases:**
- First-week quitter needing a simple daily rhythm
- Heavy smoker comparing quit apps before downloading
- Someone searching withdrawal symptoms or money saved by country

## Personas

| Persona | Cares about | Challenge | Value we promise |
|---------|-------------|-----------|------------------|
| First-week quitter | Fast relief during cravings | Urges feel endless; progress feels invisible | Visible streak + 2-minute craving interrupt |
| App comparer | Honest feature fit | Too many gamified or cluttered apps | Clean tracker focused on health + savings |
| Withdrawal searcher | Symptom reassurance | Fear that symptoms mean something is wrong | Evidence-based guides with clear timelines |

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Quitting feels abstract—willpower fades, cravings feel urgent, and progress is hard to see.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Generic advice without a daily loop
- Gamified apps that distract from health outcomes
- Trackers that are slow to open when a craving hits

**What it costs them:** Money on cigarettes, health risk, repeated failed attempts, shame after slips.

**Emotional tension:** Anxiety during withdrawal, doubt after a slip, fear it is too late to quit.

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** Kwit, Smoke Free, QuitNow, quitSTART, Quit Genius, Flamy — falls short when users want a clean visual tracker without RPG clutter or when they need iPhone-first simplicity.

**Secondary:** NRT alone, quitlines, cold turkey — falls short on cue-based cravings and daily accountability.

**Indirect:** Willpower, spreadsheets, notes apps — falls short on craving-specific tools and recovery timelines.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**
- Visible progress (streak, savings, recovery milestones)
- Fast craving interrupt (timer + one pre-committed action)
- Plain, honest voice; no fake ratings or hype

**How we do it differently:** Mechanism-first (make the quit visible) vs gamification-first.

**Why that's better:** Opens in under a minute during a craving; progress compounds daily.

**Why customers choose us:** Simple iPhone tracker that matches how they think about health, not game levels.

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| Is it free? | Yes—core tracking is free on the App Store. |
| Android? | iOS only today; Android waitlist on homepage. |
| Will this work for heavy smokers? | Works best combined with NRT/medication where appropriate; app handles cue cravings and visibility. |
| One cigarette = failure? | Slips are data, not verdicts—see our relapse guides. |
| Is this medical advice? | No—education only; talk to a clinician for personal medical decisions. |

**Anti-persona:** Someone who wants deep gamification/RPG progression (Kwit may fit better) or needs Android today without waiting.

## Switching Dynamics

**Push:** Frustration with cluttered apps, invisible progress, failed cold-turkey attempts.

**Pull:** Clean tracker, savings visibility, NHS/CDC-grounded guides, free core features.

**Habit:** Smoking cues, social smoking, reaching for cigarettes during stress.

**Anxiety:** Fear withdrawal will be unbearable; fear apps are gimmicks; fear of another failed quit.

## Customer Language

**How they describe the problem:**
- "I can't get through the first week."
- "Cravings only last a few minutes but feel forever."
- "I keep quitting and starting again."
- "How much money am I wasting?"

**How they describe us:**
- "Quit smoking, tracked simply."
- "See your streak and savings."
- "Open it when a craving hits."

**Words to use:** streak, cravings, savings, milestones, quit plan, smoke-free days, withdrawal timeline

**Words to avoid:** guaranteed, cure, miracle, addiction broken forever, clinical authority we do not have

**Glossary:**

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Slip | One cigarette after a quit attempt—not framed as total failure |
| NRT | Nicotine replacement therapy (patch, gum, lozenge) |
| Cue craving | Urge triggered by habit/context, not just nicotine withdrawal |

## Brand Voice
